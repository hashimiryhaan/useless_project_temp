# 💀 What is BrokeGPT?

**BrokeGPT** is a completely unnecessary financial roasting machine.

It scans a screenshot or live camera capture of your bank balance, uses **OCR to detect the amount**, determines your financial status, and then delivers a **random Manglish roast** accompanied by dramatic effects and audio.

Because apparently knowing your balance wasn't painful enough.
